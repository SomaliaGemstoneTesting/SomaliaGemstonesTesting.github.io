Frequently asked questions

Common questions about our services.

Find answers to questions about gemstone testing, certification, and our process.

How long does gemstone testing take? ▼

Initial assessments via WhatsApp typically take 24-48 hours. Full laboratory examinations depend on the complexity of the specimen, usually 3-5 business days.

What's the cost of testing?
▼

Pricing varies based on the type of testing needed. Contact us via WhatsApp with details of your specimen for a quote. Basic identification is affordable, comprehensive reports are reasonably priced.

Do you provide official certification? ▼

Yes. We provide detailed written laboratory reports documenting our findings. These reports help with selling, insurance, and trading purposes.

Can I get testing for stones I've already found? ▼

Absolutely! We test any gemstone or mineral specimen regardless of where it came from. Miners, collectors, and buyers use our services regularly.

How do I submit a specimen? ▼

Start by messaging us on WhatsApp with clear photos from multiple angles. We'll assess the specimen and advise on the best next step for your specific needs.

Are your assessments independent and unbiased? ▼

Yes. We provide independent analysis focused on accurate identification and assessment. Our goal is to help you make informed decisions about your stones.
